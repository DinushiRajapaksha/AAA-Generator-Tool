## 2.11.1.1 - BDR for Customer Credit Information - Modify Aging Bucket

### Description
Use this activity to modify and view the start day, end day, or aging methods of aging buckets used in Accounts Receivable and analyses.

### Act
1. Use this activity to modify and view the start day, end day, or aging methods of aging buckets used in Accounts Receivable and analyses
2. This mandatory task is to be performed by a system administrator or equivalent person if using credit management
3. Aging buckets show the value of outstanding invoices of particular ages
4. The age of an invoice is based on one of several aging methods
5. Aging can be viewed according to the following: Installment due date, installment due date excluding invoice lines with exception codes, invoice date, or invoice date excluding invoice lines with exception codes
6. If there are invoice lines with exception codes, the system will reduce the amounts of these invoices from the total invoice amount which has qualified for an aging bucket
7. Six predefined buckets are created at creation of a company
8. You cannot add or delete any of the buckets or add aging codes
9. You can redefine day range per bucket and also specify a different aging method per bucket
10. This means that you can redefine the day range for all six buckets to use one aging method, or you can redefine two buckets for each of three aging methods or any other combination of buckets and methods
